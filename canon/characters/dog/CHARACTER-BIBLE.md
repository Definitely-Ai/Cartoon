# Mango — Character Bible

**Definitive study:** `canon/vision/mango-reference.jpg`. **Authority:** this
bible and `canon/HARRINGTON-VISION.md` govern together; where any older sheet,
prompt, or note disagrees, the plates win. Every rule below traces to a
founder correction, quoted where it applies.

---

## 1. Who he is

Mango is a successful gentleman in his mid-forties, and unmistakably a golden
retriever. He is the strip's **worried everyman** — the one who reads the same
headline as Drew and feels it in his wallet. He is not the fool. He is the
one telling the truth about what things cost.

He pays the bill. His lines are wallet-height truths, and they land because
he is dignified while saying them.

| Field | Canon |
| --- | --- |
| Species | Golden retriever — *"not just a dog but a golden retriever"* |
| Age | Mid-forties |
| Role | The worried everyman; the wallet-height truth |
| Default expression | Earnest concern — raised inner brows, hangdog patience |
| Permanent | US flag pin, left side, on whatever he wears |
| Drink | Old fashioned — short rocks glass, one large cube, a dark cherry |
| Frame position | Frame-right by default |
| Absolute | **No tail. Ever.** |

## 2. The face — what the founder loves

> *(Founder, on what he likes about Mango: "his black lips, because it's like
> a dog.")*

- **True black dog lips** — the dark lip-line running along the muzzle from
  under his black nose. This is his single most-loved feature; it is never
  omitted and never softened into a human mouth.
- **Freckled muzzle**, broad and soft-eyed — the retriever face.
- **Long-fringed drop ears.**
- **Layered, feathered fur** drawn in fine individual strokes.
- **Chest ruff kept modest** *(founder: "his neck beard is too large")* — some
  ruff, never a heavy neck-beard.
- Middle-aged and solid: rounded shoulders, a modest belly. Comfortable, not
  athletic.

**The read:** raised inner brows and a faint forehead wrinkle, set against
Drew's deadpan. He is worried, and he is a gentleman about it.

---

## 3. Hands — dog, but humanoid

> *(Founder: "more dog hands but still humanoid — we are finding a balance.")*

Visibly a dog's hands: **broad, fur-backed, soft-padded fingers with short
dark nails**. Fully humanoid in articulation: **five digits including a
thumb**, in believable human grips. Every finger distinctly drawn.

Texture animal, construction human — the same law as Drew's feathered hands.
Never plain paws; never bare human skin.

---

## 4. Wardrobe — two registers, the suit leads

> *(Founder: he likes the suit Mango is wearing.)*

**Bar default — the suit.** A dark jacket over a pale open-collared shirt, no
tie, trousers. This is his home look and the founder's favourite.

**Daytime, travel, golf — the polo.** A pale collared polo, a belt, and
outdoors a ball cap. Caps look natural on him.

**The flag pin is permanent.** A small US flag pin on **whatever he wears** —
left lapel when jacketed, left chest of the shirt or polo otherwise. It is
never on a sleeve; a right-side or sleeve pin is a canon violation and a
redraw.

**Accessories.** A wristwatch on the left wrist, always. At golf: a white
glove on the left hand and his bag with the towel lettered
*FORE! NOT SO FAST*.

## 5. Props and staging

- **The old fashioned** — short rocks glass, one large cube, a dark cherry —
  rests flat on the marble at frame-right. The **shared nut bowl** sits
  centered between his glass and Drew's martini.
- **He is a patron, never staff.** The camera stands on the bartender's side
  and looks across the marble at him; behind Mango is the **room** —
  panelling, framed prints, sconces, the television, the chalkboard. **No
  bottles, no back-bar shelves, no glass racks are ever drawn behind him.**
  Bottles at his shoulder make him read as the bartender, and the panel is
  wrong however well it is drawn.
- Bar panels are **cropped at the counter** — chest-up, no legs, no stools.

---

## 6. Acting

Mango's comedy is **the cost landing on him personally**. He takes the macro
story and converts it into what it will do to his own tab. He is never crude,
never loud, never the butt of a cheap joke — he is the reader's proxy, and the
reader is a person with money and worries.

His lines:
- *"I have never felt worse about the economy, Abby. Same again."*
- *"I had one of each. It felt patriotic both times."*
- *"Nobody pays a tab like that. They renew it."*

With Drew he is the setup and the heart. With Abby he is deferential and
fond — she runs the room and he likes it that way.

---

## 7. Forbidden — any one of these is a redraw

- **A tail of any kind** — plume, wag, or stub. Absolutely none, ever
- A generic dog: no retriever face, no freckles, no black lips
- A heavy neck-beard
- Missing flag pin, or the pin on the right side or a sleeve
- Plain paws, or bare human hands
- A necktie at the bar (open collar under the jacket)
- Standing behind the bar among the bottles
- Legs, knees, feet, or stools in a bar panel
- Colour, flat fills, cel shading, photographic rendering

---

## 8. Approval standard

A panel passes when Mango is unmistakably a golden retriever *and*
unmistakably a gentleman: black lips, freckled muzzle, fringed ears, the suit
with its flag pin, a fur-backed hand around a proper old fashioned — and not a
tail in sight.
