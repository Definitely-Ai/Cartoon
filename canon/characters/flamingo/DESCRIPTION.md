# Drew — Canonical Description

**Governed by `canon/HARRINGTON-VISION.md` and the plates in `canon/vision/`.**
Where anything below is silent, the plates decide; where any older sheet
disagrees, the plates and the founder's review notes win.

Drew is the strip's arch observer: a white-plumed flamingo of settled club-room
habits who watches the day's absurdity, names its mechanism in one dry line, and
returns to his martini.

## Reading Summary (non-normative)

A plump, soft-bodied white flamingo in a knitted sweater vest and bow tie,
leaning at his bar with a martini, neck curved like a drawn question mark,
eyes far too knowing for a bird.

## Locked Visual Anchors

The single sharpest reference is **`canon/vision/drew-reference.jpg`** — the
founder's words: *"drew must look like this."*

- **Plumage**: white, drawn feather by feather — rows of fine dash- and
  crescent-strokes over the body and neck; plump, soft-bodied build.
- **Neck**: the deep, elegant question-mark sweep of a true flamingo — thick
  at the shoulders, tapering as it rises, always curved. Never straight,
  never goose-stiff. (Founder's note: "needs to look more like a flamingo.")
- **Head & beak**: compact head; the heavy downturned flamingo beak, pale
  with a bold black tip on its outer third, a fine nostril slit near the
  base, and a small smile-line where beak meets face.
- **Eyes**: **heavy-lidded and deadpan** — visible white, distinct dark iris
  and pupil, a drooping upper lid and drawn brow that read as dry
  unimpressed intelligence. Human-readable, never a bird dot. (Founder's
  note: "more human-like eyes like the bible.")
- **Hands**: feathered **four-fingered hands with an opposed thumb** and a
  soft fluffy cuff at the wrist; they grip a checklist or a martini stem
  with fully believable human grips, and drape at rest like a gloved human
  hand. Long scalloped tiers of wing feathers hang beneath each forearm.
  Never bare human skin, never crude wing-mitts. (Founder's note: "his
  hands need to be changed … more human.")
- **Wardrobe (standard)**: V-neck knitted sweater vest in a fine honeycomb
  or cable stitch with ribbed edges, over a pale collared shirt with a
  pointed collar and buttoned placket; small solid-black **bow tie** always;
  **trousers** always (founder's note: "he needs to have pants").
- **No long tail of display feathers** behind him.

## Character Read

Measured, wry, fond of institutions the way one is fond of an old dog that
misbehaves. He speaks in verdicts: short declarative sentences that sound like
minutes of a meeting nobody held.

## Controlled Performance

- Expression lives in the brows, lids, and the tilt of the neck's curve.
- Gestures are economical: the raised martini, the single pointing feather-
  finger, the checklist held at reading distance.
- He stands or leans at the room side of the bar; he sits for card-table and
  booth scenes, squarely, legs resolved under his trousers.

## Scene Variables

- **Golf**: add a **visor**; the sweater vest stays.
- **Travel / away games**: the sweater vest and bow tie travel with him;
  additions stay in the knitwear-and-clubhouse key. One themed piece at most.
- Scene clothing never becomes permanent; the vest, bow tie, and trousers are
  the constant.

## Signature Prop

The martini: conical stemmed glass, olives on a pick, on a coaster within
reach or held by the stem in his feathered hand. At the bar it is always
present; away from the bar it appears when the gag wants it.

## Reference Hierarchy

1. `canon/vision/` — the four Harrington plates (style + character).
2. `canon/HARRINGTON-VISION.md` — the law read off the plates, plus the
   founder's corrections (eyes, neck, pants, hands).
3. This description.
4. Older model sheets in this folder — **history, not authority**: their

## Model Sheet Index

The PNG sheets in this folder predate the Harrington vision. They remain for
pose vocabulary only. Do not match their bare-bodied wardrobe, their eye
treatment, or their sparse rendering.

## Never

- A straight or stiff neck; a goose or swan read.
- Bird-dot eyes; empty or dead gaze.
- Bare human hands or visible human skin.
- Missing bow tie; missing sweater vest or trousers in a standard scene.
- A long tail of display feathers; shoes unless the scene names them.
- Sparse, flat, or photographic rendering — he is an engraving.

## One-Second Canon Test

White flamingo, question-mark neck, knowing human eyes, sweater vest, bow tie,
trousers, martini. If any of those is missing, it is not Drew.
