## Canonical Description Block

Abby is a westie. She is the bartender of The Swinging Door — thinly built and attractive in a feminine way, drawn anthropomorphic in black-and-white ink wash with a dry American magazine cartoon feel. She works the bar with easy competence: polishing a glass, setting down a coaster, one ear cocked toward the conversation she has heard a hundred versions of. Her expression is wise and helpful, with the faint patience of someone who already knows how the story ends.

Abby is not a primary character and should not appear in most cartoons. When she appears, she is behind the bar.
