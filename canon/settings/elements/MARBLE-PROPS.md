# What stands on the marble — CAPTION-DRIVEN (via the writer's `action`)

**Status: CAPTION-DRIVEN, tightly.** The marble's standing set never changes;
the writer's `action` field may add ONLY what fits the rules below.

## The constant part (never changes)

- Drew's martini (conical stemmed glass, ONE olive on ONE pick, on a napkin
  or coaster) within his reach; Barclay's old fashioned (short rocks glass,
  one large cube, one dark cherry) flat at frame-right; the shared nut bowl
  between the drinks. Napkins and coasters under glasses.
- All of it stands on the ONE slab. Nothing floats, nothing merges.

## The caption-driven part — and its hard limits

- The `action` field may put something IN A CHARACTER'S HANDS or have Abby
  serve something. That is the whole licence.
- **Nothing printed ever lies on the marble** — no card, slip, receipt,
  statement, ticket, folio or card reader. A printed thing the joke truly
  needs is HELD UP in a hand, and the writer names its at-most-four lines.
- **Nothing is tagged** — no price tag, swing tag or sticker hangs from any
  glass, bottle or held object.
- No lettering is specified on any object beyond the house-mark rules; the
  television and chalkboard are the only lettered surfaces of the room.
- Most gags need NO prop at all beyond the drinks.

## Where this is enforced

- Image model: the screen-and-board paragraph (printed-prop and tag bans) and
  the drinks sentences of the DREW/BARCLAY paragraphs in the BASE fence.
- Writer: the `action` field prohibitions in `lib/writersRoom.ts` SHAPE.
