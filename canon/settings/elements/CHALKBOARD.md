# The chalkboard — CAPTION-DRIVEN (slot: `[BOARD]`)

**Status: CAPTION-DRIVEN.** The board is a constant fixture; the chalk on it
belongs to the day's joke, through one slot.

## The constant part (never changes)

- One chalkboard in a dark wooden frame, right of the television, above the
  back bar. Same board, same place, same size, every cartoon.
- **The WHOLE board is in the picture**: its wooden frame visible on all four
  sides, never cropped by the panel's edge. (Operator ruling 2026-09-01:
  "half the chalk board is gone.")
- Hand-lettered chalk serifs, real correctly spelled English, menu-shaped.

## The caption-driven part

- `[BOARD]` — the day's special, written by the writers' room from the SAME
  joke as the caption: a thing, a price, and one turn (a board with no figure
  on it is a second caption in chalk — at most one per ten).
- **THE BOARD PRICES THE JOKE — it never urges drinking hard, fast, cheap or
  often.**
- **An empty `[BOARD]` slot wipes the board clean**: bare slate, no chalk
  marks. The fixture stays; the content stands down. A lettered board the
  plan did not order is model invention and fatal.
