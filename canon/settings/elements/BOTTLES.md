# The back bar & bottles — CONSTANT

**Status: CONSTANT.** The same collection, the same shelves, every cartoon.

## The locked description

- The back bar stands ACROSS the counter on the far service side — walnut
  shelving, mirror behind, hanging stemware above — behind Abby when she is
  cast, behind no one when she is not. **Never behind the gentlemen.**
- **High shelves only:** the lowest shelf sits above standing-Abby's
  shoulders; below it, plain fielded walnut panelling. No bottle at counter
  height, no work-top ledge.
- **The bottles are the SAME collection every day**, distinct shapes,
  **visibly filled to different levels** — some near full, some half, a few
  low, the liquid line showing through the glass. A shelf of identical
  empties is a redraw.
- **Exactly TWO bottles are lettered** — BIRDIE BOURBON (left) and DIVOT
  DRIVE GIN (right) — every other bottle completely blank. *(The founder's
  "not labeled" note from the 25-panel review is an open question for him;
  until he rules, blank-by-design stands.)*
- A bottle reaches the customer side only in Abby's hand or set down by her.

## What a caption may do to it

Nothing. No gag bottle, no third label, no rearranged shelf. A drink the gag
needs arrives in a glass or in Abby's hand.
