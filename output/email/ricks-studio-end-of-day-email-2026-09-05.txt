Subject: The Swinging Door - end of day, September 5: the bar is in a good place

Hi Rick,

Today's report is attached. The short version: the scene is in a good place, and on Monday the characters go into it.

The room is now built as separate pieces, each perfected on its own and put back into the picture without disturbing the rest. That is what let us fix the things that had been bothering us. The bar's edge is dead straight again (a flaw in how the pieces were cut had put the ghost of a chair back onto it, and that is gone at the root). The back bar is the design I chose from three: two heavy walnut slabs on three posts, standing on the marble ledge with the panelled wall open behind them. The cabinet doors under the ledge are gone, the ledge runs clean into the window wall, and the window is one pane from the marble to the crown with the mirrored sign in a cleaner, classier face, lifted off the hot-dog cart. Every version of every piece is on file, so if you ever prefer an earlier shelf or bar we can go straight back to it.

The chairs and the characters are out of the picture on purpose. On Monday we recreate Drew, Barclay and Abby properly from their character bibles - friendly, each with the texture of their own animal, with eyes that read as human - in a set of poses so they can look at each other in different ways for different captions, and then seat the two gentlemen at the bar.

The report shows all the little things we perfected today, in order, including the mistakes and what we learned from them.

Talk Monday.
