## Verdict

- **Reference plate for Barclay: `barclay-reink-20-25-s3.png`.** It is the only plate that carries the head, the wardrobe (pin canton included), the furred paw on the cut-crystal glass, plain paper and a true engraved hand all at once. Its one drift — a slightly rounder near eye — is the smallest departure in the set.
- **Runner-up: `barclay-reink-20-25-s77.png`.** The best head in the set by a nose; hold it as the likeness reference and use it only where the lapel is out of frame, since its flag pin has lost its canton.
- s7 is disqualified for the signature and must not be used or re-derived from.
