It is a loose reference for WHO he is, not for the pose, the crop or the props - do not copy its background, its martini or its framing, and never draw a second bird.

THE RULES THE FINISHED PICTURE OBEYS:

A drawing made like an antique steel engraving: fine pen crosshatching and stippling on every surface - knit stitches, feathers laid in individual strokes, cloth, leather - with a full tonal range from deep rich blacks through silvery greys to bright paper highlights. Confident contour lines over the hatching. Black-and-white only; no flat fills, no cel shading, no photographic rendering, no color.

DREW is exactly the flamingo gentleman of his reference: white plumage feathered all over in fine dash-strokes with no sleeve or cuff, the long high S-curve neck and small refined head, a slender pale bill with a black outer third and a plain slit nostril, one heavy-lidded amiable eye with a clear iris and catchlight, a crisp white collar band under a small black bow tie, a V-neck knitted sweater vest with no pockets over a pale shirt, feathered hands with four fingers and a thumb and no nails. He is plump and soft-bodied, a successful gentleman in his mid-forties; his neck is thick at the shoulders and curves upward in the deep question-mark sweep of a true flamingo, never straight and never goose-stiff. He wears trousers, and on this sheet plain dark shoes, because a turnaround shows the whole figure. He has no long tail of display feathers behind him.

THE SHEET IS A CHARACTER MODEL SHEET, not a scene: bare white drawing paper, four ruled panels and nothing else. No room, no bar, no back bar, no window, no wall, no furniture, no floor, no ground line, no cast shadow, no horizon, no tone or hatching behind the figures - the paper inside each panel stays white except where the bird himself is drawn. No martini, no glass, no props of any kind in his hands. No second character, no duplicate bird inside one panel. No lettering, caption, signature, number, arrow or annotation anywhere except the four panel labels already printed on Picture 1, which stay exactly as they are.
