## Verdict

**Reference plate for Abby: `abby-reink-20-25-s77.png`.**
It is the only plate that keeps BOTH eyes built to the rule — white either side of a drawn iris, a
smaller round pupil, one catchlight, a lashed upper lid — while also holding the ears, the stippled
button nose, the closed-lip smile, the sleek throat, the studded collar with its teardrop gem, the
open blouse and the rolled cuff, on plain unwashed paper, in continuous engraved tone with no hard
outlines and no lettering anywhere on the sheet. Fix on the next pass: shorten the smile line back to
the canon's subtle half-smile, soften the iris ring, and pull the lashes back from glamour toward the
original's fine fan.

**Runner-up: `abby-reink-20-25-s3.png`** — the finest hand in the field and a near eye that matches
canon exactly, but the far eye has no pupil and the sheet carries a grey wash with a drawn bottom
edge. Usable for the coat and collar; not usable as the eye reference.

**Disqualified on marks/ground:** s7 (letter scrawl), s90 (signature), s41 (photographed sketchbook page).
