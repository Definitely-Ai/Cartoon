**Reference: `drew-reink-20-25-s90.png`.** It wins the two features that carry Drew — the long diagonal black outer third with its ridge highlight, and the plain slit nostril — while keeping the heavy-lidded amiable eye, the bow tie at exactly the original's scale, the dark olive, and feathered hands with four fingers and a thumb, all on clean bright flat paper with no mark on it. Its one departure is a slightly light print (26.0% vs 28.9%), which is a gain, not a grey to be levelled out.
