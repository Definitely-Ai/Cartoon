Constant across all six: the tight original crop is lost — the figure floats with margin on all four sides (1024x1536 in, 1344x1680 out). Wardrobe, martini with olive, and the four-fingers-plus-thumb feathered hands survive everywhere; collar and bow tie are drawn a size large in every plate but s41.

**Reference: `drew-reink-20-25-s44.png`.** s41 has the better face — its bill and its heavy-lidded eye are the only ones that read as Drew rather than as a flamingo — but it prints as a flat grey low-key plate that cannot be levelled (its whites top out at 246 against a 219 ground), so it would carry that grey into everything fed from it. s44 is a clean paper plate whose two misses are single features: soften the eye-rim and lengthen the black third toward s41. Both seed-7 plates carry a signature — a hard fail under the no-lettering rule.
