# The cast, understood - recreation plan (2026-09-05)

Founder: recreate Drew, Barclay and Abby perfectly from the bibles; multiple poses, looking at each other for different captions; Abby not always present; friendly, animal-specific texture, eyes human and huggable.

## Generation route

HYBRID — REFERENCE-GUIDED RENDER IN CONTEXT FOR THE BODY, CODE-SIDE FACE TRANSPLANT FOR THE HEAD, CODE-SIDE SHADOW. Neither pure route can work here, and the reason is in the server, not in taste.

WHAT THE SERVER CAN AND CANNOT DO (verified in backend/providers/local_bridge.py): the only drawing endpoint is POST http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/generate. References are CONDITIONING ONLY — every family builds an EMPTY latent and every sampler is hard-coded "denoise": 1.0. There is NO img2img strength, NO mask/inpaint node, NO ControlNet, NO IP-Adapter. /api/compare silently drops input_images — never use it. So the model cannot be made to keep a face; every pixel it returns is drawn from noise. That single fact decides the route.

A) BODY/POSE LAYER — reference-guided render IN CONTEXT (never on an isolated canvas: an isolated figure brings its own light, its own perspective, and engraved hatching has no solid edge to key).
- Conditioning Picture 1 = the CURRENT approved plate with ONLY this part's silhouette replaced by its own grey value block-in: canon/room-kit/v2/values/<part>.png, drawn per part with only what stands BEHIND it (this is the fix for the 2026-09-05 shared-values bug, where the counter's mask pulled 48.6% of its block-in from chair pixels and the model drew a chair into the bar).
- Picture 2 = the character's cast portrait, byte for byte the file the cast page shows (lib/generate.ts VISION_REFS): canon/vision/studies/drew.png, studies/barclay.png, studies/abby.png — cropped to a TIGHT CHEST-UP BUST at native resolution. Never a whole-scene tile: "Barclay's bar panel dominated every board it sat on, absorbing Drew into a second retriever and dropping Abby entirely."
- Picture 3 = the tight head tile at native resolution, gradient at or above its own plate (plates span 4.78-8.35): drew-plate1-head-study.jpg (335x275, gradient 8.49), barclay-face-reference.jpg, the head crop of studies/abby.png. Never drew-bar-reference.jpg (2.94x upscale, gradient 3.40 — "the model was handed a smear whose most salient dark shape was the hooked tip").
- Request body: {"prompt": <see perCharacterPrompts>, "model": "local/sensenova-u1.5", "width": 1184, "height": 1792, "fast": false, "steps": 40, "guidance": 4.0, "seed": <7|21|33|44>, "input_images": [<3 data-URIs>], "negative_prompt": <per character>, "tag": "<part>-s<seed>"}. sensenova takes up to 6 refs on HiDreamO1ReferenceImages (Picture 1..6) and feeds both conditionings; sizes snap down to a 32-px grid.
- FULL mode (40/4.0) is right here because the whole silhouette IS the subject and the plate around it is protected in code. FAST mode (8-step Lightning, cfg 1) is for the caption variants in (D), where a character is in frame and must not be touched: measured, a full-mode edit takes Drew's head from 17.1 to 55 local-laplacian, fast leaves him at 14.8.

B) FACE — NEVER RENDERED FROM NOISE AGAIN. "Fresh sensenova rolls mangle Drew's bill; the founder's approved gpt-image-2 faces are better than anything local." After the body seed is picked, transplant the head box from the approved study, scaled to the pose's head box, with scratchpad paste_face.py: ring-SAD alignment (bestOffset, +-40 px, step 2, ring = max(24, 0.4*min(w,h))), ring gain clamped 0.85-1.18, cosine feather at 22% of the box's short side. This is exactly the mechanism that held the cast in the speaker era, where 99% of every "variant" was the approved plate's own pixels. For a head at a yaw the study does not carry (DREW-05, BARCLAY-05), transplant only the EYE box and the BILL/MUZZLE box and let the render keep the skull.

C) EVERY POSE IS ONE MODULAR LAYER, filed as a room-kit part:
- canon/room-kit/v2/masks/<part>.png — the pose's own silhouette mask (mask_of(part, grow, feather)).
- canon/room-kit/v2/values/<part>.png — its own block-in, only what stands behind it.
- canon/room-kit/v2/parts/<part>.png — the approved render.
- canon/room-kit/v2/shadows/<part>.png — a SEPARATE layer: the chair-contact shadow and the counter cast shadow, solved with level_to() against WALL_TONE 112.0 / GLASS_TONE 178.0 and multiplied under the figure, so a pose can be swapped without its shadow going with the wrong body.
- canon/room-kit/v2/parts.json — one entry per pose, {"enabled": false} until the founder passes it, plus "protect": the other figure and both sconces.
- Composite with scripts/build-plate.py: polygon outline from parts.json, align() on the ring, tone_match() (gain 0.88-1.14, offset +-26), relight() by frequency separation against canon/plates/base/light-map.png (sigma 42), cast_mask()/protect subtracting the other character. Those four levers took worst drift outside a part from 7.13 strokes to 1.22.
- Part names: figure-drew-01-rest, -02-toward, -03-speak, -04-react, -05-tv, -06-abby; figure-barclay-01..06; figure-abby-01..05. Same numbering as poseSet, so a caption row in gazeMatrix names its layers directly.

D) CAPTION VARIANTS ARE PASTES, NOT RENDERS. Once a pose pair is approved, a day's caption changes only the mouth box of the speaker and the eye boxes of the listeners, pasted onto the finished plate with pasteRegions/paste_face.py boxes recorded in the pose's JSON (the duo.json pattern: drew.mouth 370,770,140x190; drew.eyes 335,700,80x50; barclay.mouth 790,850,230x210; barclay.eyes 905,800,150x70). Never re-render a whole figure for a caption — that is what put "a line through Barclay's head".

E) EYES AND TEXTURE, the founder's two asks, are won HERE and not by more prompt: the huggable human eye survives only because it comes from an approved face by paste (Abby's corrected eye exists in NO image in the repository, so hers is built once, by hand, from the §2 five-part table — white at each side, drawn iris with fine radiating lines, a smaller pupil, exactly one catchlight, defined lids — and that hand-built head becomes her paste source forever after). Animal-specific texture is won by rendering IN CONTEXT at 40 steps so the pen matches, then by naming the fault when it drifts ("the strokes have dissolved into smooth grey and the edges have gone woolly" took Barclay's skull from 20 to 54) and by a clarity pass (unsharp 0.9 + local contrast 0.35) rather than a full-mode redraw, which "replaces mush with wire".

NO PAID OR CLOUD API AT ANY STEP. All of this is 127.0.0.1:8000 -> ComfyUI 8188 plus Python/sharp on this side.

## Gaze matrix

GAZE LAW FIRST, and it governs every row: Drew §6 — "He never looks at us. His gaze belongs inside the room — at Barclay, at the television, at the chalkboard, or down at the glass… a Drew who has turned his eye down the lens is a redraw, however good the head is." Barclay §7 — "Eyes turned out of the panel at the reader" is a redraw. Abby §7 — "A gaze that meets the reader's" is a redraw, and §5: "Her body is angled into the frame and her face goes where her body goes… She never looks out of the panel."

1) CAPTION IS DREW'S, TO BARCLAY. Drew = DREW-03 (bill slightly parted, head up 40-44, eye sliding sideways at Barclay's face). Barclay = BARCLAY-02, mouth CLOSED corner up, both eyes on Drew's face, brows up. Abby (if present) = ABBY-01, head yaw -34 onto Drew, mouth closed, one hand still on her working object. Nobody's eyes leave the room.

2) CAPTION IS BARCLAY'S, TO DREW. Barclay = BARCLAY-03 (mouth open mid-word) — this is the ONE parted mouth, saved for the beat where he is actually saying the line. Drew = DREW-02 or DREW-04, bill closed, eye on Barclay; carry the head UP in at least one panel in four. Abby = ABBY-01 turned to Barclay (head yaw -30), mouth closed.

3) CAPTION IS ABBY'S / BOTH GENTLEMEN TO ABBY. Abby = ABBY-04, mouth parted on one word, gaze on the gentleman she is closing (head yaw -36), never on the lens. Drew = DREW-06 (head yaw +150, pitch -8), Barclay = BARCLAY-06 (head yaw -152, pitch -6): both looking UP and across at her, both mouths closed. Her word settles it and their eyes say so.

4) BOTH TO THE TELEVISION / CHALKBOARD. Drew = DREW-05 (head yaw +160, pitch -12), Barclay = BARCLAY-05 (head yaw -168, pitch -14) — two heads turned the same way, one a little later than the other so it does not read as a chorus line. Abby = ABBY-01 with the head yaw -50 toward the set but ONE hand still closed on a working object; she glances, she does not stop working. Whoever's caption it is takes the open/parted mouth; the other two stay closed.

5) "ASIDE TO THE READER" — NOT DRAWN, AND IT IS THE ONE ROW THE CANON REFUSES. No character ever looks out. Stage the aside instead as the TURNED-AWAY ASIDE: the speaker's head is turned OFF his addressee — Drew to DREW-01 (eye down into the martini, pitch 56, bill parted a hair) or Barclay to BARCLAY-01 with the eyes down to the glass — while the listener keeps his eyes ON the speaker. The reader gets the aside from the eyeline mismatch, not from a look down the lens. The house then typesets the caption; the fourth wall stays a wall nobody in the bar knows is there.

CROSS-ROW CONSTANTS: exactly one mouth open per panel, and it belongs to the speaker; every listener's mouth is closed (Drew's bill never opens at all except the small outer-half parting when the line is his); every gaze has a named target inside the room; Drew stays frame-left and Barclay frame-right in every row, including every row Abby is in; at least one panel in four carries Drew's head up at 40-46.

## Local reference support

EVERYTHING LOCAL GOES THROUGH ONE ENDPOINT. AuraVision (Z:\ImageGenerator, `.venv\Scripts\python.exe run_server.py`, port 8000) dispatches EVERY request to backend/providers/local_bridge.py -> ComfyUI at LOCAL_COMFYUI_URL (default 127.0.0.1:8188). backend/providers/__init__.py: "All models execute locally via ComfyUI on RTX 4090" — the dispatcher has exactly one provider ("local": True); fal.py / openai_direct.py / openrouter.py / replicate.py are dead files never reached.

ENDPOINTS (backend/main.py):
- POST /api/generate  -> GenerationRequest, returns GenerationResult {success, image_url, local_path, thumbnail_url, seed_used, metadata{seed,steps,cfg,sampler,scheduler,shift,fast,family,width,height,references}}. THE ONLY drawing endpoint.
- POST /api/compare   -> same request fanned across several model ids (NOTE: it constructs GenerationRequest WITHOUT input_images, so references are DROPPED — never use it for cast work).
- GET  /api/health, /api/models, /api/presets, /api/history; POST /api/enhance-prompt; POST /api/keys (only LOCAL_COMFYUI_URL is honoured); static mounts /static and /outputs.

REFERENCE SUPPORT — what exists (backend/providers/base.py GenerationRequest):
- input_images: Optional[list[str]] — base64 data URLs or http(s) URLs. Comment in the file: "the cartoon studio sends the character portraits and the empty set plate here, and a provider that drops them draws a different cast every time." Each is POSTed to ComfyUI /upload/image by _upload() and wired to a LoadImage node.
- For local/sensenova-u1.5 (_graph_sensenova): refs ride HiDreamO1ReferenceImages with flat inputs "images.image_1".."images.image_6" feeding BOTH the positive and negative conditioning; max_refs = 6. Prompts address them as "Picture 1", "Picture 2" (plate-desk's localize() rewrites @imageN -> Picture N).
- negative_refs (bool, default true) — whether the negative branch also sees the references.
- Other dials: fast (bool -> 8-step Lightning LoRA at cfg 1; else 50 steps cfg 4), steps, guidance, shift (SenseNovaSamplingOptions, default 3.0), sampler (euler), scheduler (simple), seed, width/height (snapped down to a 32-px grid for sensenova), aspect_ratio, negative_prompt, lora + lora_strength (extra LoRA by filename), tag. _dials(): steps==25 and guidance==7.0 mean "unset" and fall back to the model default.
- Per-family ref paths: qwen = TextEncodeQwenImageEditPlus, 3 refs; flux2 = ImageScaleToTotalPixels 1MP -> VAEEncode -> ReferenceLatent on both conditionings, 4 refs; joyimage = TextEncodeJoyImageEdit, 6 refs.

WHAT DOES NOT EXIST — verified by reading all four graph builders:
- NO img2img / denoise strength. Every family builds an EMPTY latent (EmptyHiDreamO1LatentImage for sensenova, EmptyLatentImage/EmptySD3LatentImage elsewhere) and every sampler is hard-coded "denoise": 1.0. There is no strength parameter in GenerationRequest and no way to pass one. References are CONDITIONING only; the picture is always drawn from noise.
- NO masks / inpainting. No LoadImageMask, no SetLatentNoiseMask, no InpaintModelConditioning, no VAEEncodeForInpaint anywhere in backend/. No mask field on the request.
- NO ControlNet, no IP-Adapter, no depth/canny/lineart preprocessors, no LoRA training endpoint.
- NO custom-graph endpoint: the graph is built in Python per family, so a new capability means editing local_bridge.py (add a LOCAL_MODELS entry + a family builder) and restarting AuraVision.

CONSEQUENCE: ALL masking, alignment, feathering, tone-matching, relighting and lettering is done in CODE on this side, never by the model.
- scripts/room-part.py: PNG masks under canon/room-kit/v2/masks/<part>.png; mask_of(part, grow, feather); conditioning built by pasting the part's value block-in into the current plate; posts {"prompt","model","width","height","fast","seed","input_images":[data-uri],"negative_prompt","tag"} to http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/generate (adds steps 40 / guidance 4.0 when not fast); render size 1184x1792; level_to() solves a level against WALL_TONE 112.0 on the back-wall mask (GLASS_TONE 178.0).
- scripts/build-plate.py: polygon outlines from canon/plates/parts.json, align() on the ring, tone_match() (gain 0.88-1.14, offset +-26, ring = 26-iteration dilation minus inside), relight() by frequency separation against canon/plates/base/light-map.png (sigma 42), cast_mask()/manifest.protect to subtract Drew, Barclay and both sconces from any part.
- lib/plates.ts: toPlateSize, bestOffset, pasteRegions, plus perspective warp (lib/warp.ts) onto screenQuad/boardQuad.

## What worked

- PASTE-BY-REGION IS WHY THE CAST HELD. A speaker variant keeps only the mouth box and the listeners' eye boxes; the rest is the approved plate byte for byte. Ring-based alignment (bestOffset) + a feathered rounded-rect mask means the model's inevitable few-pixel drift never shows.
- SUBTRACTIVE EDITS ARE RELIABLE. "Remove the board and leave plain panelling", "take the name off the glass — behind where those letters were the building simply continues uninterrupted" worked first time, every seed. Reshaping architecture never did.
- FAST MODE (8-step Lightning, cfg 1) IS THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT DIAL for anything touching the cast. Measured on Drew's head (local laplacian; his original reads 17.1): a full-mode window edit takes him to 55 and a full-mode marble edit takes his vest 17.5 -> 33.7, while fast leaves him at 14.8. SenseNova-U1.5 fast is the only model in the bake-off that keeps the plate's own soft hatching, so a pasted mouth box has almost no seam.
- FULL MODE ONLY WHERE THE WHOLE BAND IS THE SUBJECT AND NO CHARACTER IS IN IT: TV stills (best of all with NO reference at all), the chalk, Barclay's ear.
- RENDERING A PART IN CONTEXT, NEVER IN ISOLATION (parts.json _doc): the render is a full render of the plate and only the outline is composited back, so the part inherits pen, light and perspective for free. Drawing on its own canvas fails three ways — its own lighting (collage), its own perspective, and no usable alpha (engraved hatching has no solid edge; keying leaves halos).
- THE FOUR LIGHTING LEVERS: a written light law pasted verbatim into every part prompt, the stored light map, ring tone-matching, and protected regions. Worst drift anywhere outside a part fell from 7.13 strokes to 1.22; right sconce +7.13 -> -0.04, Drew's neck -3.28 -> -0.59, his collar -2.64 -> -0.23; his vest, Barclay's muzzle and jacket, both shelves and the chairs all exactly 0.00.
- ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH FOR IDENTITY (lib/generate.ts): the generation reference IS the cast-page portrait, same file byte for byte — "the cartoons must never generate something different from the cast."
- TIGHT BUSTS, NOT WHOLE PANELS, as references: "A tile that carries a whole scene competes with the scene being asked for: Barclay's bar panel dominated every board it sat on, absorbing Drew into a second retriever and dropping Abby entirely."
- THE 2026-09-05 FIGURE RENDER ITSELF WORKED ON THE FIRST SEED SWEEP: seed 7 for both. Drew s7 verdict "approved 2026-09-05 - vest, collar band, curved neck, beak in profile"; Barclay s7 "seed 7 approved - the pair shown to the founder", "the golden retriever's furred head with a drop ear, the muzzle in profile with the black nose, the dark suit jacket and pale collar rising out of the chair". Rendering Barclay AFTER Drew was approved, against the plate that already carried Drew, is what matched his pen and light.
- NAMING THE FAULT IN THE PROMPT fixes local defects: Barclay's woolly skull ("the strokes have dissolved into smooth grey and the edges have gone woolly") 20 -> 54; the eyeball-wrist ("redraw that spot as an ordinary furred wrist... no eye, no knot").

## eyesAndFriendliness

THE READ — the correction that matters most. Founder: "When we initially look at Mango he looks sad; the more you look the happier he gets. I'd like something to change slightly so he looks happier." The rule: "He is the worried everyman, but he is not sad, and the FIRST GLANCE must never say sad." The split that fixes it: "The worry lives in the brows. Everything below them is warm." Cheeks: "Full and lifted — the fur under the eye rises toward the ear" / do not "Flatten the cheek, or hollow it". "A viewer should read *a good-humoured man who is a little concerned*, never a downcast one. Mouth up, brows up, eyes bright." Draw the brows first and the eye second, in that order of importance.

3. "The reference out-votes the text. When three rounds of rewording move nothing, stop rewording and go look at the conditioning image."
