## 039. window-close-up-sign-three-lines-compressed-heavy-face-wider-tracking

![window-close-up-sign-three-lines-compressed-heavy-face-wider-tracking](images/039-window-close-up-sign-three-lines-compressed-heavy-face-wider-tracking.png)

**The ask.** The sign's characters are too small and too close together.
