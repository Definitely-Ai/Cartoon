THE ROOM. When the front window is in frame it carries the bar's name, mirrored as read from inside; a framed house sign may hang on the wall, handsome but not obligatory in every panel. MANGO. Middle-aged and solid, rounded shoulders, modest belly, absolutely no tail. His fixed read is the worried, earnest everyman — raised inner brows, a faint forehead wrinkle — against Drew's deadpan.
