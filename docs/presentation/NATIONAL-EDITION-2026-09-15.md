# September 15 national city presentation

## Scope

Twelve new city cartoons precede the existing Austin/Los Angeles pair and the original 38. The 17-slide web presentation is designed for a large 16:9 screen, with full original artwork and separately typeset, exact-match captions. Arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End, a slide selector, and Escape are supported. Native fullscreen has a fixed-screen fallback.

The live studio is mounted throughout the presentation so changing slides does not discard a submitted request. Enter city, state, and quantity. Its progress is based on confirmed server milestones, not an invented timer. Historical finished artwork is not automatically presented as the result of a new demonstration.

## Editorial and artwork method

The examples are **prepared, editor-curated cartoons**, not evidence of an unattended end-to-end run. Each brief records source scope, local relevance, and alternative captions in `city-showcase-20260915.json`. September 15 is the edition date, not an assertion that every source was published that day. Dialogue, drawings, and chalkboard menu prices are fictional. No audience rating is claimed.

Fresh TV pictures were generated with the local RTX 4090 FLUX pipeline under the cooperative GPU lease. No image-generation API or OpenRouter was used. Composition used the approved `barclay-reference-v2` acting plates. Protected pixels, grayscale, image hashes, caption lengths, and text bounds are checked. No original gallery image was overwritten.

Visual review rejected early LA pictures with writing or ambiguous passenger silhouettes, an early Chicago bus scene with possible people, a Houston picture with an extraneous glass/calculator lettering, and Atlanta jet-bridge lettering. The selected replacements use simple empty scenes. Final panels retain the approved cast and room, hand-lettered chalkboard, and deterministic captions.

## Rehearsal order

1. Open `/gallery/presentation` on the display and choose **Start presentation**.
2. Explain the consistent cast, local source context, and saved production queue.
3. Once worker readiness is confirmed, start **one** real cartoon on the live-demo slide. A busy GPU may require waiting; this is not an instant render.
4. Continue through the prepared city edition while the request works, returning with **Live demo** to see its actual status.
5. Finish with the cartoons, cast dossiers, print-size comparisons, and clearly labeled newspaper layout studies.
