**Three house rules you'll see at work**

The camera stands behind the bar, so the gentlemen sit across the marble with the room behind them and never the bottles — they are patrons, not staff. Nobody human is ever in the bar; the television is the only place people appear. And the TV picture and the chalkboard illustrate *that* cartoon's own joke rather than repeating a stock scene.
